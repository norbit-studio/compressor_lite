1Scope and who we are

This Privacy Policy ("Policy") explains how Norbit Studios ("we", "us", "our"), the developer of the mobile application Photo Compressor Lite (the "App"), handles information when you use the App.

The App is a utility that compresses, resizes, and converts photos and related files locally on your device. We do not operate any cloud service for the App, do not require accounts, and do not knowingly receive your photos.

For the purposes of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the UK GDPR, where any personal data is processed in connection with the App, the data controller is:

  • Norbit Studio

Note that most processing relevant to advertising is performed by Google (see Section 6), which acts as a controller for its own purposes under its own privacy policy.

3What the App accesses on your device

The App accesses the following items only on your device and only at moments you initiate an action that requires it:

3.1 Photos and image files you select

When you tap a button such as "Compress to KB", "Batch", or "Folder", the operating system presents its native picker (e.g. the Android Photo Picker on Android 13+). The App reads only the photo(s) or folder you select, in order to perform the requested operation (compression, resizing, format conversion, or archive creation). The original files remain unchanged on your device.

3.2 Output files

When the App produces a result (a compressed image, resized image, converted image, or archive), and you choose to save it, the App writes the output to your device gallery in an album titled "Photo Compressor", or to a folder you select. Output filenames include a descriptive suffix such as compressed_186KB, resized_1080x810, or webp_98KB to help you identify them later.

3.3 Local history

To let you re-open or share recent results without picking files again, the App stores a local history database on your device. Each history entry contains:

  • The file path or content URI of the output file
  • The original and resulting file sizes (in bytes)
  • The output format (e.g. JPEG, WebP, PNG, ZIP)
  • Dimensions where applicable (width × height in pixels)
  • The action performed (compress, resize, convert, batch, ZIP, folder, etc.)
  • A timestamp

The history database is kept on your device only. It is never transmitted off the device. You can clear individual entries or all history at any time from Settings → Storage, or by uninstalling the App.

3.4 App preferences

Your in-app preferences — default quality mode, default output format, theme, history limit, and similar — are stored locally on the device using the operating system's preferences API. These never leave the device.

3.5 Temporary files during processing

While processing an image, the App creates short-lived temporary files in its private cache directory. These are deleted automatically when processing finishes or is cancelled, and the App also sweeps any temporary files older than 24 hours on startup.

4What we do not collect or do

For clarity, we want to state explicitly:

  • We do not operate any backend server that receives your photos or other content from the App.
  • We do not require user accounts, sign-in, profiles, or identifiers tied to your identity.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your photos with any third party.
  • We do not use first-party analytics SDKs in the App. We rely on Google Play's aggregate, anonymous installation and crash statistics, which are provided by the platform without our additional integration.
  • We do not access your location (other than coarse IP-based estimation by Google AdMob — see Section 6), contacts, microphone, SMS, call logs, calendar, or health data.
  • We do not perform facial recognition, biometric identification, or content analysis on your photos.
  • We do not train machine learning models on your photos. No image content leaves your device.

6Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is supported by advertising. Advertisements are served by Google AdMob, a service operated by Google LLC and, in the EEA / UK, Google Ireland Limited.

6.1 What AdMob may collect

Google's AdMob SDK is embedded in the App and may collect and process the following data independently of us, for the purposes set out in Google's privacy policy:

  • Identifiers — including the resettable Advertising ID (AAID on Android, IDFA on iOS) and, where applicable, an app-set ID.
  • Approximate location — derived from IP address, not from device GPS.
  • Device information — such as device model, operating system version, language, time zone, and network type.
  • Diagnostic and performance data — for example ad-render success, ad-load times, and crash reports relating to the ads SDK.
  • Ad interaction events — such as which ads were shown to you and whether you tapped them.

We do not control which specific data Google's AdMob SDK collects. Google's handling of that data is governed by Google's privacy policy, available at https://policies.google.com/privacy. Google's policy specifically for partners can be found at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

6.2 Consent for personalized advertising

In jurisdictions where consent is required by law (including the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, and Switzerland), the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent SDK to present a consent dialog before any personalized advertising is shown. If you do not consent, you will continue to see ads, but they will not be personalized based on your browsing or app usage.

You can revisit or withdraw your consent at any time from Settings → Privacy → Manage ads consent in the App, where supported.

6.3 Resetting or limiting your Advertising ID

  • Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads → "Delete advertising ID" or "Reset advertising ID".
  • iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → toggle off "Allow Apps to Request to Track", or simply decline the App Tracking Transparency prompt for the App.

6.4 How we use ads inside the App

We follow our own internal ad UX rules to limit disruption:

  • No advertisement is shown before you see the result of a processing operation.
  • No advertisement is shown during processing.
  • Interstitial ads are subject to a frequency cap (no more than one per two to three successful exports, and no more than once every three minutes).
  • Rewarded ads are always voluntary; declining a rewarded ad never blocks core compression functionality.
  • Native ads in lists are clearly labelled "Ad" or "Sponsored" and never disguised as user content.

14Security

Because the App's content data does not leave your device, our security model relies primarily on the operating system's app sandboxing and your device's own security (lock screen, OS updates, etc.). Specifically:

  • The App stores its data within its private application storage, isolated from other apps by the operating system.
  • Temporary files are removed automatically as described in Section 13.
  • Network calls made by the AdMob SDK use HTTPS.
  • We do not store any access tokens, passwords, or credentials, as the App has no accounts.

No system is fully immune from compromise. We recommend keeping your device and operating system up to date, and being cautious when granting third-party apps access to the gallery folder used by the App.
